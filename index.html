Features

Everything happens on the lens

In‑lens display

A 600×600 full‑color display sits in the right lens — up to 5,000 nits, roughly 20° of field of view, and visible only to you.

Meta Neural Band

Reads the electrical signals your muscles send at the wrist (EMG), so a soft pinch or swipe scrolls, selects, and types — no touchscreen required.

Camera

12MP photos and 3K video at 60fps, with 3× zoom and a live viewfinder shown right on the in‑lens display.

Audio & calls

A 6‑mic array and open‑ear speakers handle calls, Meta AI, and music — without ever blocking out the room around you.

Battery

Up to 6 hours of continuous mixed use per charge, and up to 30 hours total with the pocket‑sized charging case.

Messaging & navigation

Read and reply to WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, and phone messages hands‑free, plus walking, cycling, and transit directions on the lens.
